🌏 Understanding the Australian Energy Rating Label

Under the Greenhouse and Energy Minimum Standards (GEMS) Act, most electrical appliances sold in Australia must display an official Energy Rating Label. This system allows consumers to compare the energy efficiency and running costs of competing models on a fair, standardized basis:

  • Star Rating Index: Ranges from 1 to 10 stars. Each additional star represents a significant percentage reduction in electricity usage compared to a standard benchmark model of the same size.
  • Annual kWh Estimate: The label displays an estimated annual energy consumption figure (kWh per year) based on standardized laboratory testing and typical Australian household usage patterns.
  • Standby Power Waste: Modern appliances constantly draw "vampire power" while waiting for remote control signals or network updates. Selecting high-efficiency models minimizes this passive drain.
