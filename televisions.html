From our data exploration and aggregation workflows, several important market trends emerge for Australian consumers:

  • Dominance of Value-Oriented Brands: Within this specific filtered segment of the database, value and retail-exclusive brands such as EKO (55 models), JVC (46 models), and AIWA (45 models) register the highest diversity of model variations.
  • Strong Mid-Market Competition: Brands like BAUHN (31), BLAUPUNKT (30), TOSHIBA (27), and LOEWE (22) form a competitive second tier, giving buyers substantial options across small and medium screen sizes.
