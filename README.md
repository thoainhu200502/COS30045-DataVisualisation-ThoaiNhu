# Appliance Energy

## Data Story
The target audience for this visualization is average Australian consumers who are looking to buy a new television. They are mostly interested in knowing which brands offer the most models on the market, and how the screen size and technology affect energy consumption. This helps them make cost-effective decisions.

## About Data
The data is sourced directly from the Australian Government's open data on appliance energy consumption (specifically for Televisions). I used KNIME to process the data: cleaning text capitalizations, merging inconsistent brand names, filtering for models currently available in Australia, converting screen sizes from cm to inches, and categorizing the sizes. The data is publicly available with no personally identifiable information. I also handled missing values during the cleaning process.
